About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at 🏫 Shandong University. My research interests are artificial intelligence, network science and engineering, large language models, and AI agents and their applications. My work has appeared in Nature Communications, KDD, EMNLP, and Physica A. I have two first-author papers accepted at CCF A– or CORE A*–ranked conferences or journals. I also serve as a reviewer for The Web Conference (WWW 2027).
